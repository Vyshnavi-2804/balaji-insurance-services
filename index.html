Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Can I enquire online or by phone?

Yes. You can send an enquiry through this website or contact the business by phone or WhatsApp.

Can I get help with different types of insurance?

You can enquire about the insurance categories listed on this website. Assistance is subject to the services and authorisations available.

Does an enquiry guarantee policy approval or a claim?

No. Policy issuance, coverage, pricing and claim decisions are determined by the relevant insurer under the policy terms.
